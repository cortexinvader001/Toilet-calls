For Businesses

Clean, stocked restrooms - on a schedule, not on your staff.

Monthly restroom care for businesses: inspections, sanitizing, restocking and emergency service, handled by ToiletCall.

What is included

  • Scheduled restroom inspections
  • Cleaning and sanitizing
  • Restocking of tissue, soap and paper towels
  • Odour control
  • Emergency call-outs for subscription customers

Why it matters

Customers judge a business by its restrooms. Your staff should not be pulled off their jobs to deal with a toilet emergency. We are built for restaurants, gas stations, offices, churches and small businesses.

How it starts

  1. You send the enquiry form.
  2. We do a walk-through or a call to assess the restrooms.
  3. You get a monthly plan.

Need an actual plumber?

Leaks, pipe repairs and toilet installation are plumbing jobs, and we do not do them. If your job needs one, we will tell you straight away and connect you with a licensed plumber from our partner network - so you still solve the problem with one call.

ToiletCall is a toilet cleaning and rescue service, not a plumbing company. We clean, sanitize, unclog simple blockages, remove odours and restock restrooms. For pipe repairs, leaks or installation, we connect you with a licensed plumber.

Services

  • Unclogging - no plumbing repair
  • Odour removal and deodorizing
  • Restroom restocking
  • Scheduled restroom inspections

  • Help for seniors, landlords and Airbnb hosts
