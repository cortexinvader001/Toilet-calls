About ToiletCall

A cleaning-only toilet rescue service, built for the moment a toilet becomes a problem.

01

Cleaning only, on purpose

ToiletCall is a cleaning and rescue service. Staying out of pipe work lets us focus on what matters in the moment: a clean, sanitized, usable toilet.

02

Honest about what we do

If a job needs pipe work, a leak fixed or a new toilet installed, we tell you straight away and connect you with a licensed plumber from our partner network.

03

Clean, sanitized, restocked

Every visit ends with a toilet that is clean, fresh and back in service. Hygiene and products statement: We use cleaners and disinfectants suited to toilet and restroom surfaces, wear gloves on every job, and use separate cloths for toilets and for other surfaces.
