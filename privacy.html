Privacy

Privacy notice

How ToiletCall.ca uses the details you send us.

What we collect

The details you type into our forms: name, phone, email, address or area, property type, what is wrong, notes and an optional photo. Business and property-manager forms also ask for company details. We do not use analytics or tracking tools on this site.

Why

Only to reply to your request, book and carry out a visit, and arrange a referral when a job needs one.

Who sees it

The ToiletCall owner receives form submissions by email through our form service. Card payments are taken on Stripe’s own page, and we never see your card details.

Keeping and removing your details

We keep your details only as long as we need them to reply to you, carry out your visit or subscription, arrange a referral, and meet any record-keeping the law requires (for example, accounting records). After that we delete them.

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. Email us at admin@toiletcall.ca or call (431) 340-5777, and we will deal with your request promptly.

ToiletCall is a toilet cleaning and rescue service, not a plumbing company. We clean, sanitize, unclog simple blockages, remove odours and restock restrooms. For pipe repairs, leaks or installation, we connect you with a licensed plumber.

Services

  • Emergency toilet cleaning and sanitizing
  • Unclogging - no plumbing repair
  • Odour removal and deodorizing
  • Restroom restocking
  • Scheduled restroom inspections
  • Move-in / move-out deep cleaning
  • Help for seniors, landlords and Airbnb hosts
