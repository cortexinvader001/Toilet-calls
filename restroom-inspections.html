What we do not do

If a job needs pipe work, leak repair or a new toilet installed, that is plumbing. We will not attempt it - we will refer you to a licensed plumber.

ToiletCall is a toilet cleaning and rescue service, not a plumbing company. We clean, sanitize, unclog simple blockages, remove odours and restock restrooms. For pipe repairs, leaks or installation, we connect you with a licensed plumber.

Need an actual plumber?

Leaks, pipe repairs and toilet installation are plumbing jobs, and we do not do them. If your job needs one, we will tell you straight away and connect you with a licensed plumber from our partner network - so you still solve the problem with one call.
