Need an actual plumber?

Leaks, pipe repairs and toilet installation are plumbing jobs, and we do not do them. If your job needs one, we will tell you straight away and connect you with a licensed plumber from our partner network - so you still solve the problem with one call.

Quick answers

Questions people ask first.

Are you a plumbing company?

No. ToiletCall is a toilet cleaning and rescue service, not a plumbing company. We clean, sanitize, unclog simple blockages, remove odours and restock restrooms. For pipe repairs, leaks or installation, we connect you with a licensed plumber.

Can you unclog a toilet?

Yes, when the blockage is caused by waste or paper and no repairs are needed. If the blockage is in the pipes, we will say so and refer you to a licensed plumber.

Do you install toilets?

No. Installing or replacing a toilet is plumbing work. We will refer you to a licensed plumber.

Do you offer monthly plans for businesses?

Yes. Our restroom-care subscription covers scheduled inspections, cleaning and sanitizing, restocking and emergency call-outs. See For Businesses.

What if water is leaking from a pipe?

That is a plumbing job, and we do not do it. We will tell you straight away and connect you with a licensed plumber from our partner network.
